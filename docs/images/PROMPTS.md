# The images of this repository

Every image here is **decorative**. None of them shows an output of the tool, a receipt, a
table of statuses or a screen. If an image ever seems to show a result, it is a bug of this
page, not a feature of the product: an image cannot be held to a test the way a sentence
can, so it is never allowed to carry information.

They are generated by `scripts/make_images.py`, which reads the key from the environment and
writes it nowhere. The prompt, the model and the measured cost of each file are below, so a
reader can reproduce them or object to them.

## The model actually used

`openai/gpt-image-2` was asked for. **It does not exist in the OpenRouter catalogue**,
which lists `openai/gpt-5-image`, `openai/gpt-5-image-mini` and `openai/gpt-5.4-image-2`
for that family. The substitute is **`openai/gpt-5-image-mini`**, the cheapest of the three,
because decoration asks nothing more of a model.

## What was generated, and what it cost

| File | Model | Prompt tokens | Completion tokens | Cost (USD) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `paper-rule.png` | `openai/gpt-5-image-mini` | 2 865 | 5 378 | **0.0430** |

One generation, out of a budget of eight. The budget was a ceiling, not a target: a second
image would add more than a megabyte to the archive people paste into a conversation, and
give a reader nothing they need.

Total spent: **0.043 USD**.

## The prompts, word for word

**`paper-rule.png`** — 1024 x 1024, 1 514 746 bytes:

> Minimal still life photograph, soft daylight: a few pale paper sheets layered on a calm
> surface, the top one aligned exactly against a thin metal rule. muted sage green, warm
> grey and a single terracotta accent on an off-white ground. Shallow depth of field, quiet
> and precise, absolutely no text, no letters, no numbers, no symbols, no logo, no user
> interface.

Two further prompts are written in `scripts/make_images.py` and were **not** generated:
`vellum-pleats` and `stone-grid`. They cost nothing and stay there in case a page ever needs
them.

## The three rules these prompts keep

1. **Neutral subjects.** Nothing about anyone's trade or domain. An illustration that evokes
   a profession makes a claim about who the tool is for, and that claim is not ours to make.
2. **No readable text.** An image model spells badly, and a typo in a page about checking
   precision argues against the product better than any critic could.
3. **Decorative, and nothing else.** No receipt, no status table, no invented screen. A
   reader who sees something that looks like output will read it as output.

The third rule is the one that matters. Everything else in this repository is checked by a
test; an image is checked by whoever looks at it.
